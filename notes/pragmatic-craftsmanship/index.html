Why "pragmatic craftsmanship"?

Mon, 16 Feb 2026 20:40
Tags: meta area:blog

Craftsmanship is a “spiritual” part. It’s about improving your skills. Improving you tools. To be good with your craft.

Pragmatic is because we exist in the world that has limitations. We need to account for them. We need to adapt.
