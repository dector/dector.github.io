Sat, 16 Nov 2024

First, in the last few years, I started writing many personal notes in digital format. The idea is that if I’m working on some problem and I find a solution - I will put it and/or useful references inside my personal knowledge database to avoid doing research in the future (if I face that problem again or need to share a recipe).

Second, I found more and more interesting, inspiring, and useful information in the small indie blogs, published notes, etc. This feels nice and I think I am almost obliged to share my knowledge. And even if only some parts of it will be useful - it’s still indefinitely tiny compared to tremendous sources of noise that are contributing to the Internet enshittification (e.g. bots, social network shitposting, etc).

So I’ve decided to make one more attempt at public writing - this time in the form of small sketches.
